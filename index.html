Account Deletion

Users can delete their account directly from the InterviewGPT app.

Steps to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the InterviewGPT app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

  • Your account is permanently deleted.
  • Your profile information is removed.
  • Your interview history and associated user data are deleted from our systems.
  • No personal data is retained after deletion except where required by law.
